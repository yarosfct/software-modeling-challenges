## Overview questions (the five canvas OQs)

### OQ1 — Dedicated refine / rework challenge? **partially addressed**

**What we asked:** Is there a dedicated “never rework / iterate” challenge (RQ1 gap), or is it only late-feedback stakes?

**What wave 2 showed:** Three student incidents on the same timing-and-stakes mechanism ([[S8M2]] walk-around **changes**; [[S10M3]] timed **local rewrite**; [[S7M3]] grade-locked **full redo**). S9 remembered no rewrite (thin).

**Why not a new CAT:** The variation is a **dimension of CAT5** (and its interaction with CAT9), not a separate phenomenon. Splitting would duplicate “when comments arrive relative to a locked grade.”

**What remains:** How common full redo is vs S7’s *despachado* teammate; whether walk-around on an individual project is enough to replace staged deliveries ([[S8M2]] open question).

**How to address later:** Interview other IST AMS groups about phase 1 after the grade; or observe whether walk-around comments land while the artefact can still change. Not more generic student interviews.

---

### OQ2 — Shy vs outgoing CAT6 designs? **addressed** (remainder is implication)

**What we asked:** Do shy (S4) and outgoing (S5) students need different public-feedback designs?

**What wave 2 showed:** Variation is wider than shy vs outgoing.

| Case | Access pattern |
|---|---|
| S4 | Shy; prefers private teacher channel |
| S5 | Outgoing; public lab / group board |
| S7 | Comfortable showing **models** (more than code); extra comments still asked-for / in person ([[S7M4]]) |
| S8 | Sharing easy with friends and the teacher, not course-group strangers; skipped checks from **lack of interest**, not shyness ([[S8M2]]) |
| S9 | Would hide **poor drawing**, not a wrong model ([[S9M2]]) |
| S10 | Reserved **starter**; desk walk-around easy, raising a hand is not ([[S10M2]]) |

**Why this is enough for the thesis:** CAT6 now has conditions (initiative, graded vs ungraded, public vs desk, graphic skill) rather than a two-type personality split.

**What remains:** Whether a **private checker** would recover S10’s small arrow-type doubts ([[S10M2]], [[S10M6]]). That is a design implication, not a finding.

**How to address later:** Trial a private in-lab validator with reserved students, or interview more reserved IST students about unresolved small doubts after walk-around. Do not treat S10M6 as evidence that the tool works.

---

### OQ3 — Lecturer wait-to-start vs student self-report? **addressed (conditional)**

**What we asked:** How often do lecturer “students wait to start” claims (L1/L2) match student self-reports?

**What wave 2 showed:** The claim can be true of the **room** without being true of every student ([[S7M1]]: copiers vs attack-to-survive; [[S10M2]]: reserved notebook starter). Already bounded by [[S5M3]] (drafts without waiting). [[S8M1]] is a different gate (required before taught). [[S9M1]] thin “need to see it once.”

**Why not fully closed:** No matched lecturer–student pair in the **same** UC. Wave 2 added students only.

**How to address later:** Interview the AMS / ISEL lecturer alongside students from that offering, or observe the first 10 minutes of a board lab. Recruitment of lecturers was already the bottleneck.

---

### OQ4 — Densify CAT14 beyond S6? **partially addressed**

**What we asked:** Should cross-notation translation methodology (S6-heavy) be sampled in more RE courses before densifying CAT14?

**What wave 2 showed:** CAT14 is no longer S6-only.

| Case | Variant |
|---|---|
| S6 | Lost iStar-to-UML trace; no taught bridge |
| S10 | BPMN-to-UML of an external party; “models answer different questions” ([[S10M1]]) |
| S7 | Lighter: duplicated teaching + syntax at the switch; next family easier once already modelling ([[S7M5]]) |
| S8 | Lookalike diagrams confuse until an example sits beside ([[S8M3]]) |

**Not S9** ([[S9M3]]).

**What remains:** Do lecturers teach “different questions” as **content**, or only as a fix after students force one-to-one mappings ([[S10M1]] open question)? SysML and other RE courses still unsampled.

**How to address later:** A lecturer who teaches multi-notation AMS/RE, asked specifically whether the bridge method is in the syllabus. New countries/universities would be global saturation, not this thesis.

---

### OQ5 — Student evidence for abstraction deficit? **unfulfilled**

**What we asked:** Does student talk support lecturers’ “abstraction deficit” claim (parked: L3M2, L4M7)?

**What wave 2 showed:** Nothing. No student memo volunteered “I cannot abstract” or “I do not know what to leave out” as a root deficit.

**Why it was not fulfilled:** The sampling guide forbade leading probes (“are you bad at abstracting?”). Wave 2 targeted start-gate, rework, feedback access, groups, CAT14, CAT11 — not this parked item. S9’s weak recall is not a test.

**Keep parked.** Honesty remains **interpretation** (lecturer_interp only).

**How to address later:** Non-leading incidents about choosing granularity / what to omit; or matched observation of the same task. More generic Portuguese student interviews will not close this.
