## Agreed Core Phenomenon

> Learning modelling involves making and justifying representational decisions under uncertainty. Difficulties emerge when learners lack sufficient cognitive, pedagogical, social, or technical support for those decisions.

## Agreed Axial Model

The model currently contains four central learner-response modes:

1. Defer to authority
2. Explore and justify alternatives
3. Negotiate collaboratively
4. Avoid or abandon modelling

`Defer to authority` has two variants:

- Calibration seeking
- Paralysing dependence

Mixed or shifting cases are also recognized.

Familiar-pattern reuse is a cross-cutting strategy:

- Reflective pattern reuse
- Unreflective pattern reuse

## Agreed Refinements and Negative Cases

- Starting difficulty is common but uncertainty can migrate to construction, refinement, integration, evaluation, or maintenance.
- Public critique is useful only when feedback is substantive, psychologically safe, and socially tolerable.
- Domain choice is beneficial when bounded by scope, fairness, workload, access, and teaching capacity.
- Semantic tools help when validation benefits exceed interaction and prerequisite burdens.
- Continuous assessment helps when it creates manageable feedback-revision cycles.
- Formality should be proportionate to purpose and can be introduced progressively.

See the `negative_case_*.md` notes in this folder.

## Main Cross-Case Conclusions So Far

- Worked examples support independence when they expose rationale and fade assistance.
- Model-quality judgement develops through actionable criteria, feedback, comparison, justification, and revision.
- Progressive tool guidance is preferred over constant or prescriptive automation.
- Modelling value is socially produced through shared use, ownership, negotiation, and maintenance.
- Domain design is a contextual trade-off, not a universal choice-versus-standardization rule.
- Cross-family modelling requires explicit translation and traceability support.
- Modelling feels meaningful when purpose, audience, and downstream use are visible.
- Ambiguity becomes manageable through criteria, alternatives, rationale, and revision.
- Workload and attendance affect access to modelling support, but lecturer explanations of absence require student confirmation.
- Multiple feedback channels should provide equivalent substantive learning value.
- AI should augment explanation, comparison, and critique while learners retain responsibility for abstraction and justification.
