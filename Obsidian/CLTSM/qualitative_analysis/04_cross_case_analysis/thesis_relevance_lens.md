# Thesis relevance lens (for findings map)

Source: thesis TeX — mainly `2-MainMatter/chapter1.tex` (aim/RQs/contributions) and `chapter4.tex` (STGT/methods focus). Background/related-work chapters (2–3) supply topic vocabulary, not findings claims. Chapter 5 is work plan only.

## What this thesis is trying to answer / show

1. Build an **empirically grounded account** of modelling education **challenges** and **support needs** in higher education.
2. Treat modelling as a **socio-technical** activity: people + practices + notations/tools + course context (STGT-informed GT), not only individual cognition or only tools.
3. **RQ1:** What challenges do students and instructors perceive, and how do they show up across **interpreting, creating, refining, and evaluating** models?
4. **RQ2:** What **strategies** do they use, and what **pedagogical** and **tool-related support** do they say they need?
5. **RQ3:** How do **tools, course design, domains, and feedback practices** shape learning/teaching experiences?
6. Include **both student and instructor** viewpoints, grounded in concrete modelling tasks/artefacts.
7. Connect difficulties to **conditions** (tools, assessment, domains, feedback), not only to “hard notation.”
8. Secondarily: implications for teaching design and educational tool requirements — but the findings map privileges **what was found in interviews**, not design wishlist as proven effects.
9. Bloom’s taxonomy is a **sensitising** communication aid only — not a coding template or map taxonomy.
10. Gap addressed: integrated view across contexts linking challenges ↔ support ↔ socio-technical conditions.

## Relevance filter (use on every candidate category)

| Tag | Keep on overview map if… | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **keep** | Speaks directly to RQ1–RQ3; recurs across cases or is a clear student↔lecturer pair; plain-language challenge, strategy/support, or shaping condition | Hard to start; feedback timing; semantic vs drawing tools; domain motivation; group vs solo modelling |
| **maybe** | Related but thin, single-case, or mainly a design wish without clear challenge link | One internship anecdote; specific vendor tool name without a pattern; AI integrity policy alone |
| **park** | Off thesis aims for this pass; biography; jargon theory talk; saturation claims; pure literature | Programme year trivia; “theoretical coding family”; final theory claims |

**Rule:** Overview categories must be **keep**. Parked items stay off the overview (or one peripheral note at most). Maybe items can sit in the guide as thin/open, not as core CAT nodes.

## Keywords / themes for category naming (from TeX)

Prefer everyday titles that still echo these TeX themes:

- Challenges / difficulties / stuck points
- Interpreting problem text → creating models → refining → judging quality
- Strategies / coping / how people start
- Support needs: examples, scaffolding, feedback, assessment framing
- Tools: semantics/validation vs drawing; friction; guidance
- Course design / workload / attendance / assessment stages
- Domains / authenticity / motivation
- Feedback practices (when, public vs private, peer)
- Student view vs instructor view (label honestly; do not merge as one voice)
- Socio-technical (people + tools + teaching + conditions together)

## What “counts” as on-topic for the map

On-topic = interview-backed patterns about **learning/teaching software or conceptual modelling** that help answer RQ1–RQ3.

Off overview = anything that does not help a "outsider" understand challenges, support, or socio-technical shaping for this thesis.
