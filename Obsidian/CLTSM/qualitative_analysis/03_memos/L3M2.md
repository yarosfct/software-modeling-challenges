# L3M2 — OCL rigor trades off against accessibility

- Case: L3
- Codes: L3-C05, L3-C07, L3-C10
- Turns: T025, T027, T029, T035

## Idea
Executable models required OCL, which L3 names the biggest difficulty and a steep learning curve. Without OCL, constraints could not be checked; with it, a large share of students found the course demanding. L3 still insists abstraction must be trained, not outsourced—even as LLMs could now write constraints.

## Why it matters
Semantic rigor in tooling sets a floor on what the course can ask, but also filters who persists—a socio-technical selectivity effect in large cohorts.

## Open question
How do strong versus struggling students talk about OCL: as notation, as logic, or as grading gate?
