# S2M7 — scope choice as beginner coping strategy

- Case: S2
- Codes: S2-C12, S2-C24, S2-C27
- Turns: T034, T078, T088

## Idea
As a beginner on FreeCol, S2 chose an easy modelling scope within the rules rather than a mandated hard part. Familiar domains would ease starting; each student made an individual model, so group work did not deeply co-construct diagrams.

## Why it matters
STGT: student agency over scope and domain partially compensates for framework and codebase constraints. Individual deliverables limit peer modelling support even in team projects.

## Open question
When teachers assign fixed modelling targets on large systems, do students report more frustration than when scope is self-selected?
