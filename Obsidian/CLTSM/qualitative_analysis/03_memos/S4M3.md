# S4M3 — shy students need private feedback channels

- Case: S4
- Codes: S4-C07, S4-C08
- Turns: T015, T017, T033

## Idea
Design ambiguity (multiplicity, class vs attribute) is resolved in office hours or email, rarely in front of the class. Public presentation of half-finished diagrams feels stressful; quick private feedback would enable earlier sharing. The student explicitly asks for complete worked examples in materials so preparation can happen privately before targeted questions.

## Why it matters
Social format of courses—public vs private validation—shapes modelling participation as much as notation instruction.

## Open question
Are there lecturer practices (role-play, standing groups) that reduce shy-student exposure, or do they increase it?
