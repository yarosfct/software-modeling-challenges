# S2M5 — tasks need a concrete communicative purpose

- Case: S2
- Codes: S2-C29, S2-C28, S2-C10
- Turns: T028, T098, T100

## Idea
Generic "model this system" tasks felt like one chore among many without clear purpose. S2 wants assignments that specify what message or aspect to show stakeholders—e.g. model to demonstrate a particular design decision. More curriculum emphasis on modelling is also requested.

## Why it matters
STGT: task framing links people (stakeholder audience), pedagogical intent, and representational choices. Without a communicative goal, modelling competes weakly with other graded deliverables in a multi-task project.

## Open question
Do students given narrower modelling briefs (specific message) report higher motivation even on unfamiliar domains?
