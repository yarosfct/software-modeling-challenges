# L4M1 — students arrive coding-first; value clicks late

- Case: L4
- Codes: L4-C01, L4-C02, L4-C10, L4-C30, L4-C31
- Turns: T015, T031, T067

## Idea
The lecturer sees master's students enter Requirements Engineering after a broad Software Engineering course with little modelling depth. Many initially treat models as pointless next to code, and only later—often in companies—recognize why requirements and modelling matter. Undervaluing persists as a "boxes and arrows I cannot compile" mindset.

## Why it matters
For STGT, this is a curriculum-timing and practice-alignment problem: people, prior courses, and workplace context shape when modelling becomes meaningful—not just notation skill.

## Open question
Do student cases (e.g. S4) report the same late shift, or do some never reach that "click" even after industry exposure?
