# S3M2 — professor feedback as correctness gate

- Case: S3
- Codes: S3-C07, S3-C18, S3-C13
- Turns: T021, T029, T030, T032, T047

## Idea
Even with a personal symbols-to-words checklist, S3 usually waited for professor feedback before feeling a BPMN model was correct. When he missed class and relied on slides alone, uncertainty returned; a colleague filled part of that gap. For ambiguous choices he often commits to the first plausible option—partly after a databases exam where second-guessing turned a correct model wrong.

## Why it matters
Correctness assurance is socially and pedagogically distributed: live teaching, room circulation, and peers—not just individual reflection or static materials. Course conditions (attendance, slide completeness) shape whether students can self-validate.

## Open question
How do lecturers (e.g. L1) describe the same feedback gate, and do they assume students can self-check earlier than students actually do?
