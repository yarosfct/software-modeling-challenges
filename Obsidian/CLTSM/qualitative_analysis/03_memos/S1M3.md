# S1M3 — group conditions displaced modelling effort

- Case: S1
- Codes: S1-C09, S1-C10, S1-C11
- Turns: T022, T025, T027

## Idea
In a six-person team with uneven contribution, sprint enforcement felt like an arbitrary extra step. After frustrations, S1 mostly avoided modelling and shifted to finishing implementation work. A smaller team—and a shared model clarifying roles—are suggested as fixes.

## Why it matters
STGT: Agile course structures and group composition interacted with modelling practice; modelling dropped when delivery pressure and coordination failed. People and process conditions can sideline diagrams even when their communicative value is acknowledged elsewhere.
