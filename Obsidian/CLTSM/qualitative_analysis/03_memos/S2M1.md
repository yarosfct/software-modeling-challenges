# S2M1 — thinking harder than drawing

- Case: S2
- Codes: S2-C21, S2-C13, S2-C04
- Turns: T014, T036, T066

## Idea
S2 separates tool ease from modelling difficulty: drawing is largely solved, but deciding what to put where—and representing relationship nuance in arrows and lines—remains the hard mental work. Diagrams still help when concepts exceed working memory.

## Why it matters
STGT: tool features (templates, warnings) cannot replace representational judgment. The bottleneck sits in the person–notation–problem fit, not the drawing surface—relevant when evaluating tool-centric interventions.

## Open question
Do students who avoid formal frameworks (work/dissertation) report less relationship-representation struggle because they invent informal notation?
