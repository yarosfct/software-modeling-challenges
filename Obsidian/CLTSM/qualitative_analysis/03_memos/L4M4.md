# L4M4 — granularity and consistency stay hard to teach

- Case: L4
- Codes: L4-C13, L4-C17, L4-C20, L4-C15
- Turns: T034, T038, T040, T044

## Idea
Even with quality criteria lists, peer review, and rules of thumb, students struggle to judge enough refinement and consistent linkage across complementary model views. The lecturer prioritizes meaning over symbol shape and expects iteration—including crossing models out—while professionals still rely on experience for abstraction level.

## Why it matters
Model quality is a socio-technical judgment practice, not a notation checklist; teaching must address when to stop and how views fit together.
