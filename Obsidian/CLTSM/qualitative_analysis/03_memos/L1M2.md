# L1M2 — whiteboard worked example as standard scaffold

- Case: L1
- Codes: L1-C06, L1-C05, L1-C16
- Turns: T018, T020, T055, T016

## Idea
L1's core scaffold is a live whiteboard walkthrough: interpret the case, map statement by statement (e.g. pools, organisation perspective, implicit messages), then assign a similar exercise for solo work. Iteration with tutor feedback and counter-examples (e.g. why not detail the client's internal process) gradually improves deliveries.

## Why it matters
Teaching practice tightly couples instructor demonstration, semantic rationale, and immediate re-application. Tool checking is not the primary scaffold—live modelling discourse is.
