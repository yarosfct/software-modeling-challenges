# L1M6 — two-stage assessment rewards correction

- Case: L1
- Codes: L1-C09, L1-C10, L1-C08, L1-C05
- Turns: T029, T031, T033, T035, T016

## Idea
Assessment runs in two graded stages with feedback before and after each delivery; completeness, consistency, and readability are explicit criteria. L1 compares solutions in class and accepts multiple valid models when semantics hold. Not all students use dedicated feedback sessions before delivery, yet those who iterate through trial-and-error with tutor feedback improve BPMN models by the end.

## Why it matters
Feedback loops are built into course design, but uptake is uneven. Multiple-valid-solution stance shapes how students experience "good enough"—closer to S3's ambiguity tolerance than a single answer key.

## Open question
Do students describe the two-stage process as enabling confidence, or still rely on informal room feedback more than structured delivery stages?
