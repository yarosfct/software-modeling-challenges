# S2M2 — examples and professor check substitute for process

- Case: S2
- Codes: S2-C14, S2-C11, S2-C17, S2-C15
- Turns: T030, T038, T040, T048, T050

## Idea
Without an explicit step-by-step method, S2 studies completed models, copies relation reasoning, starts with objects then relationships, and finishes with trial-and-error plus a professor sanity check ("Does this look all right?").

## Why it matters
STGT: demonstrated exercises and ad hoc instructor validation stand in for missing procedural pedagogy. Learning is socially mediated (professor gate) and example-driven rather than rule-driven.

## Open question
Is the professor check experienced as formative feedback or mere approval—and does that differ when students do not initiate contact?
