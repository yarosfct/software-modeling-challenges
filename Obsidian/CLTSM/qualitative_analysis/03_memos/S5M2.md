# S5M2 — examples and public feedback calibrate detail

- Case: S5
- Codes: S5-C01, S5-C04, S5-C05, S5-C08
- Turns: T007, T011, T013, T019

## Idea
S5's main difficulty is not starting models but calibrating granularity—especially in sequence diagrams where notation and detail explode quickly. Teacher examples that contrast bloated and minimal solutions teach what to leave out; internally, "good enough" means teammates can read the diagram, not maximal completeness. Sequence diagrams also felt like a reset after ER because time ordering was unfamiliar.

## Why it matters
Teaching choices (worked examples, simplification norms) interact with tool notation affordances and prior course experience. Without explicit calibration, confident students over-model and learn through embarrassing public correction rather than pre-emptive guidance.

## Open question
Do lecturers in other cases explicitly teach simplification, or is over-modelling corrected only reactively in labs?
