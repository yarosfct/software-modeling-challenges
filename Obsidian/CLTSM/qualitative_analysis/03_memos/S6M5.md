# S6M5 — integrity-sensitive AI and semantic checking wish

- Case: S6
- Codes: S6-C11, S6-C13
- Turns: T025, T029

## Idea
S6 used ChatGPT once to explain an iStar dependency in plain language—not to generate submission diagrams—because the course warned about AI and grading focused on orally defended justification. They are more interested in AI that semantically compares a model against a natural-language brief than AI that draws boxes. Motivation comes from well-bounded organisational problems where success means a coherent model, not from "exciting" domain stories.

## Why it matters
Course integrity policies and assessment format (oral defence) shape how AI enters the modelling workflow. Like S5, S6 points toward checker/validator tools as the useful AI role, aligned with traceability-focused RE pedagogy rather than generative shortcuts.

## Open question
Is the explain-not-generate AI pattern consistent across integrity-aware courses, and do students distinguish semantic checking from generation unprompted?
