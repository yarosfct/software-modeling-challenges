# S10M3 — timed local rewrite taught a boundary self-check

- Case: S10
- Codes: S10-C09, S10-C10
- Turns: T033, T035, T037, T039

## Idea
Phase feedback caught a mixed BPMN perspective: an extra pool for something inside the organisation, and message flows that should have been sequence flows. With time left before the next delivery, the group did a large local rewrite, not a full abandon. After that, S10 started asking who is external before drawing. Notation nags were memorised; conceptual comments changed later work.

## Why it matters
Unlike S7’s grade-then-feedback full redo, S10 had time to act and the error was structural, not a rushed teammate. CAT5 is not only “too late”: when a loop exists, conceptual feedback can become a new start habit.
