# L4M5 — whiteboard compensates for missing integrated tools

- Case: L4
- Codes: L4-C16, L4-C26, L4-C27, L4-C28, L4-C29
- Turns: T039, T057, T061, T062, T064

## Idea
Without affordable, integrated, semantics-aware tooling across goal-oriented and object-oriented families, the lecturer relies heavily on whiteboard discussion, manual consistency checks, and family-specific tools like piStar for iStar only. Switching tools mid-course creates friction; the top realistic improvement would be a lightweight validator that bridges paradigms.

## Why it matters
Tool ecology (licenses, semantics, integration) directly constrains what can be taught, checked, and iterated in class—a core STGT pairing of technique and infrastructure.

## Open question
Do students contrast semantic lab tools with permissive sketch tools the way S4-C14 does?
