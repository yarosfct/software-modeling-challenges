# L2M1 — getting started independently is the hard part

- Case: L2
- Codes: L2-C08, L2-C09, L2-C10
- Turns: T027, T033, T035

## Idea
L2 locates student difficulty less in notation and more in the first move from a problem statement to a model. Students may grasp actors in theory yet stall when naming them in a case, and worked examples stop helping once the domain shifts slightly. Semantics and refinement sit together in that early-to-middle stretch.

## Why it matters
For STGT, the bottleneck is socio-technical: it is not only conceptual but tied to how tasks are framed, how much autonomy is expected, and whether transferable scaffolding exists when the domain changes.

## Open question
Do student cases show the same “theory yes, concrete case no” gap when identifying actors and actions?
