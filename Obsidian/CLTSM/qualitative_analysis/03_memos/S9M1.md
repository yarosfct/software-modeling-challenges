- Case: S9

- Turns: T007, T009, T011, T015

## Idea
S9 could not recall a stuck modelling incident; concepts felt basic. What is clear is dependence on a first worked path: the postgraduate teacher solved, then explained notation and why. Without that, S9 looked for YouTube or online examples, then reread the text. Covid-online degree classes made shared exercise-solving messy, so that lab culture is mostly missing from this case.

## Why it matters
CAT1/CAT3 here is a thin confirmation: starting is gated on a demonstration, including an informal web substitute when the teacher path is absent. Weak recall means this should not densify IST or FCT lab claims.
