# L2M4 — peer star-rating as lightweight quality check

- Case: L2
- Codes: L2-C15
- Turns: T052, T054, T056, T058

## Idea
L2 publishes student models for class-wide comparison and asks peers to rate with stars against named criteria. The lecturer sees motivation and sometimes stricter peer judgment, but also friend-bias and contest dynamics.

## Why it matters
Peer review substitutes for scarce lecturer time on evaluation, yet introduces social bias—a socio-technical workaround under resource limits.

## Open question
Do students articulate criteria during star rating, or mainly react aesthetically/socially?
