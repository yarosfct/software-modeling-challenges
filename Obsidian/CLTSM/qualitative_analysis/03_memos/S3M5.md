# S3M5 — theory slides should carry complete examples

- Case: S3
- Codes: S3-C21, S3-C22, S3-C02
- Turns: T062, T047

## Idea
S3’s strongest teaching suggestion is to connect theoretical slides to practical work with complete, correct examples before labs introduce less familiar syntax. He credits repeated contact with BPMN across courses more than simply lengthening one block. Incomplete slide examples left him unsure when learning alone after a missed session.

## Why it matters
Material design links people (student, professor, peer), pedagogy (theory then lab progression), and artefacts (slides, exercises). This is a student-proposed redesign of how course conditions sequence exposure—not an observed intervention effect.

## Open question
Do lecturers already believe slides are sufficient for solo catch-up, or do they explicitly design against slide-only learning?
