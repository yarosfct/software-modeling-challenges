# S6M4 — pair structure and notation-specific sharing comfort

- Case: S6
- Codes: S6-C06, S6-C07, S6-C10
- Turns: T015, T017, T023

## Idea
S6 works best in pairs: split ownership by diagram type (iStar refinements vs early UML drafts) with weekly mutual review, avoiding free-riding seen in larger groups. Sharing comfort is medium and notation-dependent—willing in UML labs, reluctant to present unfamiliar iStar on the board unless confident. When iStar allows multiple defensible decompositions, they document alternatives, pick one, and justify it, though they still prefer teaching-team confirmation in office hours.

## Why it matters
Group size, division of labour, and notation familiarity interact: the same student can be open in one lab format and guarded in another. Pair syncs function as a lightweight review institution that substitutes for public board risk.
