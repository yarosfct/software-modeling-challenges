# L4M6 — active discovery pedagogy resists passivity

- Case: L4
- Codes: L4-C21, L4-C22, L4-C25, L4-C32
- Turns: T046, T048, T055, T067

## Idea
The lecturer refuses to front-load solved examples, uses icebreakers so shy students cannot wait all semester, and builds solutions gradually from photographed board work while admitting not knowing everything. That contrasts with students overwhelmed by continuous evaluation who rush task-to-task without assimilating.

## Why it matters
Teaching stance (discovery, openness, pacing) interacts with course load and classroom social format—relevant when shy students prefer private feedback elsewhere.

## Open question
Does S4's preference for complete worked examples before labs (T033) reflect the same need icebreakers and gradual board work try to address from the teacher side?
