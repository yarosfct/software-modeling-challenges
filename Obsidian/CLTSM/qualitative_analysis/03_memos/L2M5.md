# L2M5 — domain and course rhythm gate modeling uptake

- Case: L2
- Codes: L2-C17, L2-C18, L2-C19, L2-C22
- Turns: T029, T067, T069, T075, T077

## Idea
L2 treats domain understanding as the hardest condition and assigns one shared domain for fairness after free choice produced uneven projects. Modeling sits mainly in one software-engineering unit where students show low interest. Attendance drops around local social peaks and cross-subject workload spikes.

## Why it matters
Modeling learning is conditioned by curriculum placement, calendar pressure, and domain access—not only classroom technique.

## Open question
Does a motivating domain story (as in L3) change perceived interest when modeling stays in a single unit?
