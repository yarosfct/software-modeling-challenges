# L1M3 — missing theory class breaks slide-only catch-up

- Case: L1
- Codes: L1-C14, L1-C15, L1-C22
- Turns: T047, T051, T077

## Idea
L1 reports students skipping theoretical sessions, then learning from slides alone, misinterpreting concepts, and arriving at practicals unprepared. He does not repeat theory in labs and resists making slides a full tutorial. He attributes absences partly to Portuguese student culture, heavy multi-course workload, and continuous evaluation pressure—not lack of interest in modelling alone.

## Why it matters
Course structure assumes theory attendance; practicals "just use" concepts. Socio-organisational conditions (semester load, evaluation design) undermine that assumption and create a theory–practice gap independent of notation choice.

Does S3's slide-only difficulty after one missed MSP session mirror the pattern L1 describes at scale?
