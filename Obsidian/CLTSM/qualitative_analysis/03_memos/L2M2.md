# L2M2 — time pressure shrinks the evaluation loop

- Case: L2
- Codes: L2-C04, L2-C05, L2-C16, L2-C21
- Turns: T015, T017, T065, T083, T087

## Idea
L2 teaches evaluation criteria and runs staged projects with feedback, but class time and ECTS allocation do not match the full cycle of explain, practice, feedback, and improve. Students are seen as skipping self-evaluation, and intermediate revisions are encouraged but not graded. The lecturer’s top realistic ask is more time—not more notation or more families.

## Why it matters
Assessment design and institutional workload shape what “good modeling” can mean in practice; tools alone cannot close a loop the course cannot afford to run.

## Open question
Do lecturers who grade revisions differently report more student self-evaluation, or does time remain the binding constraint?
