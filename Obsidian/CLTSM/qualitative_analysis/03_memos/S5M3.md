# S5M3 — early sharing and self-initiated drafts replace waiting

- Case: S5
- Codes: S5-C04, S5-C07, S5-C14
- Turns: T009, T017, T033

## Idea
S5 rarely waits for a teacher demo: they draft version one, share work-in-progress in lab and group chat, and treat peer feedback as the safety net. Early public sharing reduces anxiety because errors surface early—though S5 acknowledges this pressure could harm shy peers. They want more assessed weekly diagram milestones with peer comments to institutionalise what their team already does informally.

## Why it matters
Socio-technical conditions (assessment timing, lab culture, chat channels) shape modelling pedagogy as much as notation teaching. A student who initiates drafts and shares early experiences a different course than one who waits for authoritative solutions.

## Open question
Is early-sharing comfort tied to personality, prior lab culture, or specific course assessment design across student cases?
