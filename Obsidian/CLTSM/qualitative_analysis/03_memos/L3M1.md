# L3M1 — executable models as learning and grading infrastructure

- Case: L3
- Codes: L3-C03, L3-C04, L3-C06
- Turns: T021, T029

## Idea
L3 built assessment around USE so models could be instantiated and run—making behavior visible and gradable. The tool choice prioritized semantic checking over convenience; executable UML turned static diagrams into something students could test against scenarios like an ATM or vending machine.

## Why it matters
Execution links modeling pedagogy to verifiable outcomes at scale, shifting part of evaluation from subjective diagram reading to runnable checks.

## Open question
Do students describe execution as clarifying semantics, or mainly as a grading hurdle?
