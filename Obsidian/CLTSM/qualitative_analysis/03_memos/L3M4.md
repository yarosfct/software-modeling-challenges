# L3M4 — scaled labs: autonomy with timed rescue

- Case: L3
- Codes: L3-C02, L3-C14, L3-C15, L3-C16
- Turns: T017, T047, T049, T051, T053

## Idea
With hundreds of students and a six-person teaching team, L3 promotes small-group autonomy first, then breaks problems into pieces or runs a whiteboard demo when most are stuck. Labs are support, not a second lecture—Portuguese students rarely pre-study, so skipping theory cannot be fully recovered in lab.

## Why it matters
At scale, intervention timing and role boundaries (lecture vs lab) become design constraints as much as diagram choice.
