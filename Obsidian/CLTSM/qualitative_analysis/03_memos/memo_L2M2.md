TITLE: Starting independently remains the main barrier
DATE: 09/04/2026
INTERVIEW / SOURCE: Lecturer interview 2
RELATED RQ(s): RQ1, RQ2, RQ3
RELATED CODES: starting independently as main difficulty; semantics and refinement difficulty; idea-to-model expression difficulty; extracting actors and actions from cases; theory-to-case and cross-domain transfer fragility; example-seeking when stuck; insecurity leads to withdrawal
CATEGORY / THEME: Learning challenges

1. What is happening here?
The lecturer repeatedly returns to the idea that students struggle most when they have to begin modelling on their own. Even when they know concepts in theory, they have trouble identifying relevant elements in a concrete case and expressing them properly in the model.

2. Why is this analytically important?
This suggests that the core difficulty is not only notation recall. The harder threshold is moving from a problem statement to a first workable representation without teacher-led demonstration.

3. What seems to be the underlying issue?
The problem appears procedural, semantic, and affective at the same time. Students may know what an actor is, for example, but still fail to identify one in context, especially when the domain changes or when they feel insecure.

4. What evidence supports this?
- "Getting started independently"
- "Mainly semantics, and also refinement"
- "they struggle to express that properly in the model"
- "identifying verbs, actions, or actors"
- "they need to look at other examples"
- "some of them leave the classroom because they do not feel comfortable"

5. What tensions / contradictions appear?
Students are expected to work autonomously, yet many appear to need examples or teacher intervention before they can begin.

IMPLICATIONS:
Support for modelling should likely address first moves, example transfer, and confidence-building, not just concept explanation.

OPEN QUESTIONS:
Which kinds of prompts or scaffolds help students move from a text to a first model? Are some diagram types harder to start than others?
