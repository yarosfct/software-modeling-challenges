# S5M5 — group modelling assumes an outgoing participant

- Case: S5
- Codes: S5-C02, S5-C15
- Turns: T007, T035

## Idea
S5 volunteers to present in labs and treats group whiteboarding as a social skill as much as a technical one. Public board work exposed gaps they could not answer on synchronous messages and lifelines—embarrassing but useful. They note courses often assume everyone wants to collaborate aloud, which helps people like them but can hurt quieter students.

## Why it matters
STGT requires attending to who the pedagogy is optimised for: lab formats that reward volunteers embed personality and social risk into modelling learning, not just cognitive difficulty with notation.

## Open question
Do quieter student cases describe avoiding board presentation, and how do lecturers scaffold participation without relying on volunteers?
