# L3M6 — LLM as semantic mirror, not author

- Case: L3
- Codes: L3-C07, L3-C09, L3-C22
- Turns: T029, T033, T071, T073

## Idea
L3 sees LLMs as disruptive to prior teaching—students could now photograph models for interpretation—yet proposes feedback that explains a student’s model back in natural language for comparison with intent. Syntactic checking exists; semantic matching (including OCL vs requirements) is the gap. Auto-writing constraints would bypass learning.

## Why it matters
Emerging AI tooling reframes what must be taught vs what can be offloaded, without removing the need to train abstraction.

## Open question
Do students already use LLMs as interpreter, author, or critic—and with what trust?
