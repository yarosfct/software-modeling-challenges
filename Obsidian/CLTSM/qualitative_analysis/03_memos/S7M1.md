## Idea
IST practicals unlocked work with a simple board example, a class-built requirements list, then a student at the board while the teacher stayed quiet until a student debate finished. S7 started immediately to have something if randomly picked, and because not trying drew more teacher displeasure than a wrong attempt. Classmates often waited and copied the board.

## Why it matters
The same lab ritual produces two student strategies: attack-to-survive versus wait-to-copy. Lecturer “students wait to start” claims can be true of the room without being true of every student.
