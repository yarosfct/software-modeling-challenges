# S5M1 — team coordination carries the modelling work

- Case: S5
- Codes: S5-C03, S5-C06, S5-C09, S5-C13
- Turns: T009, T015, T021, T029

## Idea
S5 treats modelling as a group coordination practice before it is an individual notation exercise. The team reads briefs aloud, assigns a drafter, holds weekly diagram stand-ups on a shared Miro board, and resolves disagreements through prototypes rather than waiting for authority. External readers (teaching assistant, club coordinators) push the group toward stakeholder vocabulary on use cases.

## Why it matters
For STGT, people and course social structures—not only UML syntax—shape whether modelling succeeds. A live shared tool plus habitual stand-ups embed diagrams in ongoing team work; modelling failure or success is tied to group engagement and review rhythms, not just personal skill.

## Open question
Do quieter students in other cases describe the same stand-up pattern, or does S5's outgoing facilitation style mask coordination costs others bear?
