# S10M6 — wants a reused case study and a private checker

- Case: S10
- Codes: S10-C15, S10-C16, S10-C17
- Turns: T053, T061, T063

## Idea
S10 prefers oral “why” to a written “incorrect relationship.” The realistic teaching change is one case study carried from requirements through process, UML, maybe SysML, with reuse vs rethink made explicit, plus a private validator (invalid relation, uncovered requirement, contradiction across models)—not a generator. The point is feedback while this delivery can still change.

## Why it matters
These are design implications grounded in the translation incident and in public-ask avoidance. Do not treat them as tested tool effects.

## Open question
Would a private checker reduce walk-around need, or would S10 still wait for the teacher at the desk for conceptual “why”?
