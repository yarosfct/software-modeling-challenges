# S3M6 — layout choices when others will analyse the model

- Case: S3
- Codes: S3-C20, S3-C19
- Turns: T054, T058, T056

## Idea
S3 downplays domain motivation and external real-world impact for most coursework, but for his thesis eye-tracking study he deliberately spaced elements for readability because others would analyse traces. Motivation mainly affected speed; he still checked quality when work felt obligatory.

## Why it matters
Audience and downstream analysis can pull modellers toward clearer layout even when “real use” does not. STGT attention to tools (eye tracker constraints) and social purpose (experimenters/participants reading diagrams) shapes modelling behaviour beyond grades.

## Open question
When do students shift from grade-driven correctness to audience-driven readability—and is that visible outside thesis/research contexts?
