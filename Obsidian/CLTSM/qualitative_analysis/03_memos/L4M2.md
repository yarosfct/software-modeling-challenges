# L4M2 — vague problems and stakeholder work teach discovery

- Case: L4
- Codes: L4-C03, L4-C04, L4-C05, L4-C18, L4-C19
- Turns: T016, T017, T018, T042

## Idea
The course deliberately uses short, ambiguous problem statements so students must elicit requirements from real or role-played stakeholders rather than invent or receive them fully formed. Whiteboard peer review and in-class stakeholder–developer role-play extend that into collaborative validation, mirroring company practice at small-class scale.

## Why it matters
Socio-technical pedagogy here ties modelling to communication and organizational context, not diagram drawing alone. Different interview paths legitimately produce different specifications.
