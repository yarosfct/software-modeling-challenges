- Codes: S9-C04, S9-C05, S9-C06

## Idea
“Good enough” was stopping when nothing remained to add; no remembered rewrite from feedback. Public sharing was hypothetical and would be avoided because of poor drawing, not fear of a wrong model. The recommended UML tool was archaic and slow (menus like unused Photoshop) but still better than pencil, and it offered templates and explanations.

## Why it matters
CAT6/CAT8 variation: hesitation can be graphic skill plus tool UI, not shyness about being wrong (S4) or board-victim pressure (S7). The later AI-checker wish is a design implication, not lived AI use in this interview.
