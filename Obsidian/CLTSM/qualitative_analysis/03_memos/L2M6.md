# L2M6 — collaboration lives outside the modeling tool

- Case: L2
- Codes: L2-C14, L2-C07
- Turns: T023, T050

## Idea
Visual Paradigm handles modeling work, but project coordination runs through separate tools (Trello, Notion, Azure boards). Real-time co-editing inside the modeler is what L2 misses most. Separately, some students will not begin until the teacher demonstrates—or they leave.

## Why it matters
Split tooling and teacher-dependent starts are joint people–tool–practice conditions; collaboration friction may reinforce passivity in labs.

## Open question
Do student teams report the same tool split, and does it delay first model commits?
