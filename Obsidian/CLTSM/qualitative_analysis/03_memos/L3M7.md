## Idea
For BPMN at master’s level, L3 values Signavio’s cooperative editing, traceability, and in-environment feedback—features less central in the UML/USE stack, which lacks simulation in the academic version. BPMN is richer than an “extended activity diagram” joke suggests; research-workflow examples motivate graduate students.

## Why it matters
Socio-technical fit varies by modeling family: execution matters for UML behavior models; collaboration and observation matter for process modeling.
