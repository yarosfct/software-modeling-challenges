# L4M7 — abstraction gap and human requirements beyond AI

- Case: L4
- Codes: L4-C24, L4-C33, L4-C34, L4-C35
- Turns: T052, T068, T070, T077

## Idea
The lecturer sees students favor concrete compile-test work over abstract modelling, sometimes using abstraction in programming without recognizing it. Soft skills—empathy, informal trust-building—matter for eliciting requirements AI cannot replicate in client relationships. Post-AI, human communication remains the differentiator even as tools generate imperfect models or code.

## Why it matters
STGT here links cognitive abstraction, interpersonal practice, and emerging AI tooling as intertwined conditions for requirements work—not separate "soft" and "hard" skills.

## Open question
How do students describe AI's role in modelling (e.g. S4-C13) against lecturers' claims about irreplaceable stakeholder interaction?
