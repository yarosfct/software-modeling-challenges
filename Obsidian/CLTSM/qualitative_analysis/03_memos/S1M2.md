# S1M2 — worked examples bridge text and diagram

- Case: S1
- Codes: S1-C13, S1-C23, S1-C15
- Turns: T031, T033, T071, T073

## Idea
S1 learns text-to-diagram mapping by trial and error, starting from components then placing actors. Worked examples—"this description translates to this model"—are named as the most useful feedback form. Templates for common steps would also help.

## Why it matters
Without explicit process teaching (see S1-C15), example-driven learning becomes the main scaffold. STGT: pedagogy (examples, templates) substitutes for missing procedural instruction and couples learner strategy to course feedback design.

## Open question
When lecturers provide demos, do students experience them as examples (mapping) or as implicit process to copy— and does that differ by case?
