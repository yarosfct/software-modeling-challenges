# S2M4 — feedback gated by student initiative

- Case: S2
- Codes: S2-C25, S2-C26, S2-C19
- Turns: T080, T082, T084, T086, T054

## Idea
Detailed modelling feedback was available only if S2 approached teachers; otherwise grading dominated and models were a small part of final discussion. Staged feedback could help, though teacher time is acknowledged as a constraint. Quality rules were probably taught but not memorised.

## Why it matters
STGT: feedback is optional and initiative-dependent, coupled with modelling's low weight among project tasks (code smells, etc.). Assessment architecture shapes whether representational quality is learned or merely checked at the end.
