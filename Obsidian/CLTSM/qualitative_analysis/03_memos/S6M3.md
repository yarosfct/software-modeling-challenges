# S6M3 — examples and attendance carry abstraction norms

- Case: S6
- Codes: S6-C04, S6-C12
- Turns: T011, T027

## Idea
Worked examples taught S6 how many actors are enough, when to stop refining goals, and how NFR qualities link to softgoals and design decisions—without them, qualities would stay as bullet points. Tutorial attendance mattered: slides alone were weak for NFR refinements, and missing one tutorial forced painful reconstruction from PDFs. Separate piStar and UML tools added friction despite helpful semantic checks in piStar.

## Why it matters
Teaching resources (partial slides vs tutorial examples) and tool integration are course conditions that determine whether abstraction norms are learnable. A missed session becomes a socio-technical bottleneck, not a individual lapse.

## Open question
How many student cases report irrecoverable learning loss from missing a single tutorial versus catching up from slides?
