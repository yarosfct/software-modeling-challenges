# S1M1 — notation and tool overload block starting

- Case: S1
- Codes: S1-C07, S1-C08, S1-C12
- Turns: T018, T020, T029, T051

## Idea
On the FreeCol project, dense notation and a feature-rich tool stacked together. Many symbol choices created doubt about the "proper" arrow or relation before any modelling could begin. StarUML's interface felt like too much at once for simple entity-to-box work.

## Why it matters
STGT: the difficulty is not cognition alone but a socio-technical bundle—course task, UML notation, and StarUML options jointly raised the entry cost. Tool design and notation load shape whether students can start at all.

## Open question
Do other students who switched to simpler tools (e.g. draw.io) report the same "hard to begin" pattern, or does notation doubt persist even with fewer options?
