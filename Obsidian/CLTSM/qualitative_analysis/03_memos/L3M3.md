# L3M3 — typed tools instead of free drawing

- Case: L3
- Codes: L3-C12, L3-C13
- Turns: T039, T041, T043

## Idea
L3 rejected pen-and-paper and freeform drawing (e.g., Visio) in favor of strongly typed tools that disallow invalid connections. Learning was meant to happen through doing inside constrained environments, not by sketching unconstrained diagrams.

## Why it matters
Tool semantics enforce what “counts” as modeling practice early—preventing habits that look correct but violate typing rules.

## Open question
Do students ever miss sketching freedom for early exploration, or do constraints feel supportive from the start?
