# S1M6 — incremental teaching would demystify diagrams

- Case: S1
- Codes: S1-C24, S1-C04, S1-C15
- Turns: T008, T035, T087, T089

## Idea
Modelling feels useful up to a point, then arbitrary—partly because teaching felt like "here are the concepts, get to it" without remembered steps. S1 proposes incremental exercises, ideally evolving one system so context is preserved.

## Why it matters
STGT: curriculum pacing and task sequencing may explain when modelling shifts from helpful to "extra documentation." Incremental, context-preserving tasks could align pedagogy with how S1 already thinks (components first, readability over completeness).

## Open question
Would incremental same-system tasks reduce the "arbitrary after a point" feeling, or does that stem mainly from notation/tool overload on large projects?
