# S7M4 — initiative-gated feedback and split-by-notation peer review

- Case: S7
- Codes: S7-C11, S7-C12, S7-C13
- Turns: T027, T029, T031

## Idea
S7 was comfortable showing models—more than code—because modelling felt too abstract to be “that wrong.” Extra feedback still did not fall from the sky: you went in person, except after required phase meetings. Moodle was rarely used because IST students gatekeep solutions. For the group, exclusive ownership of one notation family was treated as fatal for the defence, so they split drafts by notation and peer-reviewed.

## Why it matters
Public-board comfort (S7) is not the same as access to detailed feedback. Course channels (in-person only, unused Moodle) and assessment (oral defence) shape both who gets comments and how groups divide modelling work.

## Open question
Does a shyer IST student (S10) still get phase feedback, or does initiative-gating plus board culture leave small doubts unresolved?
