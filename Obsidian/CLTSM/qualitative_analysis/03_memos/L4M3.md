# L4M3 — domain freedom trades lecturer workload for fairness

- Case: L4
- Codes: L4-C06, L4-C07, L4-C08, L4-C11
- Turns: T026, T027, T028, T033

## Idea
Letting groups choose or refine domains—scoped when too simple or complex—increases marking and feedback work but avoids imposing uninterested domains the lecturer remembers as unfair. Modelling is reserved for problems complex enough to need communication beyond a weekend build. Under semester overload, some students still ask for tighter "just tell me what to do" tasks.

## Why it matters
Course conditions (freedom, fairness, workload, concurrent evaluations) co-shape whether modelling feels worth the effort for both teacher and learners.

## Open question
Do student self-reports link domain interest to modelling effort the same way (compare S4-C15)?
