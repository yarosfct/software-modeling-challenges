# S4M4 — domain interest and audience make models matter

- Case: S4
- Codes: S4-C11, S4-C14, S4-C15
- Turns: T021, T027, T029

## Idea
Interesting domains (rare-book rules, thesis architecture) motivate careful refinement; generic shop or football-style tasks drain polish while notation may still be understood. Knowing a supervisor would read diagrams prompted clearer layout, captions, and communicative intent—modelling as message, not exercise artifact.

## Why it matters
Motivation and intended readership connect people, problem context, and representational effort—parallel to lecturer fairness concerns about imposed domains (L4-C07).

## Open question
Do other students without thesis supervisors report the same shift when models address a real reader?
