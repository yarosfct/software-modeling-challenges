\chapter{Analytic audit trail}
\label{app:audit_trail}

This appendix reproduces a \emph{selection} of analytical memos and open-code notes, not the full first-cycle corpus (277 codes, 78 memos). The selection is the memos named in Table~\ref{tab:cat_rq_summary} plus one code note per major cluster and the excerpts used in Table~\ref{tab:coding_example_students_compact}. Portuguese interviews are quoted from the English working transcripts used in analysis. Identifying course titles were already generalised in the vault notes.

\section{How to read the cards}
\label{app:audit_legend}

Each \textbf{memo} card stores one analytic idea. Fields: identifier (\texttt{CaseMn}), case, linked open-code IDs, transcript turn IDs (\texttt{T00n}), the idea, and why it mattered for category development. Memos are not findings by themselves; categories in Chapter~\ref{cha:findings} rest on comparison across memos.

Each \textbf{code} card stores one open code: a short title, case, turn IDs, and a verbatim excerpt. Codes stay close to the participant account. Student evidence, lecturer interpretation of student behaviour, and design wishes were kept distinct in later category work (Chapter~\ref{cha:methodology}).

Turn identifiers mark substantive transcript turns. They are not timestamps and were not invented after the fact.
