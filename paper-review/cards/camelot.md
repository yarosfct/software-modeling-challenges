# CaMeLOT (+ Bloom precursors)

**Use:** Learning-outcome / Bloom scaffolding for conceptual & domain modelling.  
**Also covers:** Bogdanova & Snoeck *Domain Modelling in Bloom*; Bork Bloom CM/metamodeling framework.

## CaMeLOT (Bogdanova & Snoeck)
- Design-science artifact: **Bloom reinterpreted for conceptual data modelling (CDM)** plus a **content-area / scaffolding tree** (object → class → attributes → associations → complex models).
- Provides **example LO tables** across content areas (including quality and metacognition).
- Expert evaluation of the framework; responds directly to gaps found in the precursor study.

## Domain Modelling in Bloom (precursor)
- Applies revised Bloom to books, MOOCs, exams.
- Gaps: **no metacognitive LOs**, **Evaluate** almost missing, **procedural** knowledge thin.
- Motivates CaMeLOT’s CDM-specific Bloom cells and scaffolding.

## Bork — Bloom for CM & metamodeling
- Maps Bloom knowledge/cognitive dimensions onto modelling-method components (language, semantics, notation, procedure, mechanisms).
- Smart City teaching-case evaluation: strong on Create/Apply/Analyse; weak explicit **Evaluate** and **metacognition**.
- Useful as a second Bloom lens for auditing teaching cases/courses.

## Thesis takeaway
Use as related frameworks for LO design and for arguing that modelling courses under-teach evaluation and reflective judgement — without treating them as your empirical findings.
