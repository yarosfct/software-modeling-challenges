# Maslov — Conceptual Modelling Education

**Use:** Related work — CM education landscape and gaps.

## Problem
Conceptual modelling education research is scattered across venues and notations; hard to see cumulative progress on *how* CM is taught and assessed.

## Method
Bibliometric / content-oriented review of CM education literature; maps topics, methods, and social structure of the field.

## Findings that matter
- CM education is **fragmented** (UML, ER, BPMN, domain modelling, etc. treated separately).
- Stronger coverage of notation/syntax teaching than of **evaluation, reflection, and metacognition**.
- Empirical classroom studies exist but **shared learning frameworks** are still thin.
- Tools and feedback mechanisms are discussed less systematically than content.

## Thesis takeaway
Cite as evidence that modelling education needs clearer pedagogical frameworks and more attention to judgement/evaluation — the space your interview study speaks into.
