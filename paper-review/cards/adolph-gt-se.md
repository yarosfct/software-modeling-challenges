# Adolph, Hall & Kruchten — Using GT to study software development

**Use:** Methodology chapter — concrete Glaserian GT in SE.

## What it offers
- Clear GT process model: indicators → concepts → categories/properties; constant comparison; theoretical sampling; memoing; core category.
- Practical advice on interviews, observation, tooling (stay close to data), ethics/access.
- Rigor via trustworthiness + Glaser’s fit/workability/relevance/modifiability.
- **15 guidelines** for novice GT researchers in SE.

## Thesis takeaway
Primary “how GT looks in SE” companion to STGT/Stol. Adapt practices to your STGT stance; do not copy their core category (“Reconciling Compromise”) — it is their substantive theory, not yours.
