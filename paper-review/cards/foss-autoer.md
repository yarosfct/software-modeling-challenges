# Foss, Urazova & Lawrence — AutoER / SIGCSE 2022

**Use:** Related work for **CAT5 / PH2** (immediate automated feedback on UML database-design diagrams).

## Design
Classroom system for generating and marking UML database-design questions with on-demand feedback; student satisfaction vs traditional marking delay.

## Thesis takeaway
**Validate** that delayed diagram feedback is a recognised bottleneck. **Contradict** “the only fix is more lecturer time.” Scope is database-design UML, not the multi-family courses in this thesis.
