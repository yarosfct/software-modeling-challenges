# Buchmann et al. — Conceptual Modelling in Education (position)

**Use:** Framing — what students should understand modelling *to be*.

## Core positions
- Models are **knowledge structures** (schema-conformant), not mere drawings.
- Modelling languages are **engineered, adaptable knowledge schemas**, not fixed vocabularies only.
- CM value is **co-created** by modellers, method engineers, and domain experts.
- Prefer a **Purpose–Domain** characterisation of languages over a crude GPML vs DSML split.
- Supported by OMiLAB / AMME infrastructure narrative.

## Thesis takeaway
Conceptual ammo for arguing that teaching must emphasise semantics, purpose, and language design — not diagramming alone.
