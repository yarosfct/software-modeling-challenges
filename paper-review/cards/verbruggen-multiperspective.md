**Use:** Related work for **CAT14 / PH1-adjacent**.

## Design
Observational think-aloud (n=6): same case as UML class diagram then BPMN process model; questionnaire on multi-perspective understanding.

## Findings that matter

- Understanding which requirements belong in which perspective may correlate with model quality.

**Validate** “models answer different questions.” Small n; MERODE/course-specific. Does not document iStar→UML or BPMN-pool→class translation incidents (S6, S10).
