# Domain diversity, motivation, and inclusion in modelling education

**Use:** Course design / authenticity / motivation implications.

## Design
Parallel surveys (~90 students, ~22 educators) on domain choice, assignment design, inclusion, feedback.

## Findings that matter
- Educators often assume **technical/game domains** motivate; students prefer **personal interest** and **socially relevant / everyday** domains.
- Unfamiliar or culturally specific domains can feel **exclusive**; choice/personalisation helps.
- Gamification and group work motivate but can backfire if competitive or poorly scaffolded.
- Feedback valued when students see **visible course change**.

## Thesis takeaway
Design-implications list for modelling courses: student-centred domains, careful collaboration scaffolding, responsive feedback — triangulate with your interview themes on domains and authenticity.
