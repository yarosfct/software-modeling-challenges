# Maslov — Towards empirically validated process modelling education (BPMN)

**Use:** Meta related work — agendas that link pedagogy, quality, and feedback.

## What it is
Research agenda (not yet full empirical results) for BPMN education via design science: review → novice behaviour/quality studies → validated LOs, course designs, feedback (incl. possible simulation/adaptive support).

## Thesis takeaway
Shows the field moving toward systematic, empirically grounded modelling pedagogy. Position your interview study as complementary empirical work on learner/teacher challenges across modelling contexts, not as a BPMN LO framework.
