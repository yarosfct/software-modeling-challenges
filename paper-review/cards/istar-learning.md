# Li et al. — Facilitating iStar learning (empirical)

**Use:** Closest published peer for **learning challenges in a modelling language**; methods peer (GT + Bloom).

## Design
Empirical study with iStar learners; combines error analysis, challenge elicitation, grounded coding paradigm, and derived tool/teaching requirements; prototype tool discussed.

## Findings that matter
- Recurring **error types** and **learning challenges** for goal modelling novices.
- Strategy space / coding paradigm explaining how learners cope with difficulty.
- Requirements for tooling and teaching support derived from the data.

## Thesis takeaway
Cite as empirical precedent for studying modelling learning difficulties qualitatively and linking challenges to support needs. Contrast: your study is broader (UML/BPMN/etc., lecturers + students) and not iStar-specific.
