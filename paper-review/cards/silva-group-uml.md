# Silva et al. — Group vs individual UML (SBES 2018)

**Use:** Related work for **CAT9**.

## Design
Empirical classroom comparison of individual vs group UML exercises; correctness and completeness of diagrams and use-case specs.

## Findings that matter
- Groups are not uniformly better: some artefacts more correct individually, more complete in groups.
- Syntax remained difficult in both conditions.

## Thesis takeaway
**Validate** CAT9’s two-sided claim. **Contradict** “group work is always the pedagogy.” Does not cover code-first pressure, exclusive notation ownership, or defence audience (this thesis).
