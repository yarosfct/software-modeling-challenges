# Hasker & Rowe — UMLint (ASEE 2011)

**Use:** Related work for **CAT5 / PH2** (delayed instructor feedback on UML).

## Design
Tool paper: automated defect checks for student UML, motivated by days-long delay between drawing and graded critique.

## Findings that matter
- Standard tools give little quality feedback; students wait on instructors.
- Syntactically acceptable diagrams can still be semantically wrong.

## Thesis takeaway
**Validate** late-feedback problem. Does **not** address CAT6 access. Automated linting is a design response, not evidence that checkers were used in this corpus.
