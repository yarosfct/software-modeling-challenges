# Cámara et al. — ChatGPT and UML (SoSyM 2023)

**Use:** Related work for **CAT13 / PH3**. Not a finding of this thesis.

## Design
Experience report assessing ChatGPT on UML class diagrams with OCL (syntax, coverage, prompting, translation, domain sensitivity).

## Findings that matter
- Generation is less reliable than code generation: syntactic/semantic defects, inconsistency, scaling limits.
- Authors discuss LLMs as modelling assistants, not as replacements for modelers.

## Thesis takeaway
**Relate / mild validate** student explain-not-generate stance. **Contradict** any claim that LLMs already supply a compile button (CAT12/PH2). Do not treat as evaluation of the four CAT13 cases.
