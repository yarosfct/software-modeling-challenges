TITLE: Different modelling families demand different ways of thinking
DATE: 19/03/2026
INTERVIEW / SOURCE: Lecturer interview
RELATED RQ(s): 
RELATED CODES: cross-model interference; modelling paradigm shift; variability-oriented thinking; optional-vs-mandatory confusion; notation complexity
CATEGORY / THEME: Learning challenges

1. What is happening here?
The lecturer notes that students struggle when moving between modelling families, especially from SysML/UML-style system modelling to feature modelling for families of systems.

2. Why is this analytically important?
This suggests that modelling difficulty is not only about notation syntax. It may involve shifting conceptual frames, objects of representation, and reasoning styles.

3. What seems to be the underlying issue?
Students may carry assumptions from one modelling paradigm into another. The difficulty is framed as a mindset shift rather than only a language-learning issue.

4. What evidence supports this?
- “the interference of the models”
- “move from uml or sysml... to a feature model”
- “family of systems”
- “optional features or mandatory feature”

5. What tensions / contradictions appear?
A multi-model curriculum gives broader exposure, but may also increase interference and conceptual overload.
