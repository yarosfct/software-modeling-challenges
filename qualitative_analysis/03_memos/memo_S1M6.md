MEMO ID: S1M6
TITLE: Authentic and personally meaningful domains increase modelling effort
DATE: 23/03/2026
INTERVIEW / SOURCE: Student interview 1
RELATED RQ(s): 
RELATED CODES: authentic project-based modelling; domain interest boosts motivation; visible impact motivates modelling; authenticity increases engagement; choice increases effort and quality care
CATEGORY / THEME: Engagement / motivation

1. What is happening here?
The student responds positively to modelling when it is tied to a meaningful, real project and a domain he finds interesting. He says he puts in more effort and cares more about clear diagrams when the project matters to him.

2. Why is this analytically important?
It suggests that student engagement with modelling is not only about the notation or pedagogy. Domain relevance and authenticity shape whether modelling feels worth the effort.

3. What seems to be the underlying issue?
Motivation appears stronger when students can see the impact of their work, recognize the artifact as real, and care about the problem space.

4. What evidence supports this?
- "go into the code base... and then extend the game"
- "because I do like video games"
- "having something where I can immediately see my impact... makes it much more motivating"
- "it's such a long standing project... it was a lot of fun"
- "I'll go out of my way to make it really good"

5. What tensions / contradictions appear?
The same project that was motivating through its authenticity was also frustrating through team dynamics and process overhead.

6. How does this relate to other interviews or literature?
This is useful to compare with the lecturer's attention to familiar case studies. Here, the student perspective sharpens that idea into interest, ownership, and visible impact.

7. Implications
Course design may benefit from domains that feel real, consequential, and optionally aligned with student interests, especially if modelling is meant to be perceived as worthwhile.

8. Follow-up questions
Does allowing students to choose domains consistently improve modelling engagement? How much authenticity is enough to affect effort and quality?
