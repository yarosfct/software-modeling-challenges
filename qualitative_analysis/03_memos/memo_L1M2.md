RELATED CODES: example-based teaching; step-by-step modelling demonstration; worked example then independent task; counterexample-based support; rationale-based feedback
CATEGORY / THEME: Teaching support / scaffolding

1. What is happening here?
The lecturer teaches modelling through examples, whiteboard walkthroughs, and procedural demonstration. Students first observe a worked example, then attempt a similar case by themselves.

2. Why is this analytically important?
This shows that the lecturer is not just transmitting modelling concepts, but demonstrating a modelling process. That is important because modelling competence may depend on seeing how experts move from a problem statement to a model.

3. What seems to be the underlying issue?
Students need support in proceduralizing modelling activity. They benefit from seeing the sequence of choices and the reasoning behind them.

4. What evidence supports this?
- “Its by example”
- “you go step by step”
- “i give the example... then i give them another one for them to do on their own”
- “Give counter examples”
- “trying to explain giving rationale why you are doing this”

5. What tensions / contradictions appear?
The course aims to build student independence, but strong teacher guidance seems necessary early on.
