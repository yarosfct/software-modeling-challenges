TITLE: Modelling difficulties are shaped by workload, attendance, and course context
DATE: 19/03/2026
INTERVIEW / SOURCE: Lecturer interview
RELATED RQ(s): 
RELATED CODES: attendance decline; missing concept introduction; self-study from slides; classroom knowledge beyond slides; workload pressure; continuous assessment overload; student culture explanation
CATEGORY / THEME: Contextual factors

1. What is happening here?
The lecturer attributes some student struggles to missed classes, overloaded schedules, and institutional assessment structure rather than only to modelling itself.

2. Why is this analytically important?
It broadens the explanation of modelling learning difficulties beyond individual student deficits or notation complexity.

3. What seems to be the underlying issue?
Learning opportunities are mediated by attendance, timing, workload, and access to live explanation that is not fully captured in slides.

4. What evidence supports this?
- “students start disappearing”
- “they dont go to the theoretical classes”
- “they try to learn by themselves through the slides”
- “not everything is there, i discuss that in the class”
- “heavy workload”
- “continuous evaluation is not good”

5. What tensions / contradictions appear?
The lecturer values student autonomy, but also sees student absence as damaging to learning.
